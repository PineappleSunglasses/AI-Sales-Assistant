# Case 1 Summary

## Business Problem

Rohde & Schwarz runs an annual budget process across approximately 72 ISOs and
200 sales offices. Budget V1 submissions are collected in Excel, consolidated,
and manually checked against planning expectations, division structures, and
business-plan corridors.

## Pain Points

- Regions plan without direct access to confidential five-year business-plan
  targets.
- Budget V1 is often a discussion starter rather than a usable plan.
- Manual synchronization across ISO files and division dimensions takes weeks.
- Divisions use different planning lenses, especially product versus segment.
- Functional-unit planning is integrated late.
- Structural changes such as renamed or merged segments break comparisons.

## MVP Output

- Upload area for budget files and planning premises
- Normalized budget database
- Validation findings with severity, affected dimension, current value, expected
  range, and rationale
- AI-assisted correction proposals
- Dashboard for Raphael's team
- Review list focused on the highest-priority escalation cases
- Validated Excel export
